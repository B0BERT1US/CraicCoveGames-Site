We are CraicCove Games, a three person indie studio in Omagh, Northern Ireland. This is the new home for everything we are working on.

Our first game is Identity Protocol, a tactical FPS where you play a police robot being trained to replace the human force. Every mission is a test, and how you handle it decides whether the experiment survives.

We will post dev updates here as we go. If you want to know when the game launches, the newsletter signup on the home page is the place.
